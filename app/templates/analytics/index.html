The COINVAULT Growth Score Formula

We reject arbitrary black-box rating algorithms. The Growth Score evaluates player discipline, consistency, and yield on a normalized scale from 0 to 100:

Growth Score = (30d % Growth × 0.40) + (Consistency Factor × 0.35) + (Streak Bonus × 0.25)
1. Growth Rate (40%) 0 – 40 pts

Evaluates relative net yield over rolling 30 days. Scaled linearly up to 100% gain cap to prevent single speculative anomalies from distorting the score.

2. Consistency Factor (35%) 0 – 35 pts

Derived from the Standard Deviation of daily changes relative to mean gain. Punishes volatile binge-spending and rewards steady accrual.

3. Streak Bonus (25%) 0 – 25 pts

Rewards uninterrupted consecutive days of active non-negative ledger growth (10 pts per day, capped at 100 pts normalized).
